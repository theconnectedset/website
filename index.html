Privacy Notice

THE CONNECTED SET LIMITED PRIVACY NOTICE FOR CONTRIBUTORS TO OUR PROGRAMMES

This Privacy Notice explains what personal data we will hold about you, how we collect it, and how we will use and may share information about you when you provide contributions to us. It is for on-air talent (such as actors, musicians, presenters, writers and composers) and other contributors (such as interviewees or those providing material like photos or the use of their property as a filming location), and individuals who have previously contributed to our productions.

1. Who collects information about you?

The Connected Set Limited (“Connected Set”) is a ‘data controller’, responsible for deciding how we hold and use certain information about you.

2. What information do we collect about you?

You provide certain information as part of your contribution: your image, voice, photos, views of your property and other identifying information. You also provide information in your contract and correspondence, including your name, email address and address, and if we pay you, your banking details.

3. How do we use your information?

We process your information for the purposes of our business, including production, broadcasting, distribution and marketing. As a data controller we rely on legal bases including the performance of our contract with you, our legitimate interests (managing and administering our business effectively), your consent (for example for marketing contact), and compliance with our legal obligations. This covers your participation and its assessment, remuneration and bank details, contacting you and your representatives, enabling the creation, sale and distribution/broadcast of productions (including sharing with the relevant Commissioner, the rights management platform Soundmouse and distribution agents), diversity and equal-opportunities monitoring, physical and system security, disputes and legal proceedings, and day-to-day operations.

4. Who will you share my information with?

We may share your information internally with employees, managers and administrators (including finance) working on your production. We will only share with third parties outside our group where consistent with a legal basis and where lawful and fair, for example external recipients of emails containing your information, aggregated and anonymised diversity data to regulators on formal request, and footage, images or audio of you for broadcasting, distribution and marketing (or to credit your role).

5. Transfers of information outside the UK

In connection with our business and for production, broadcasting, distribution, administrative, management, marketing and legal purposes, we may transfer your information outside the United Kingdom (UK). This includes transfers to members of our group and to data processors in the US and, on occasion, other jurisdictions in which we are established. Some of our systems are hosted outside the UK.

Where we transfer your information to a country that the UK has not deemed to provide an adequate level of data protection, we ensure a similar degree of protection is afforded to it by putting in place appropriate safeguards, such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the standard contractual clauses. Transfers to the European Economic Area (EEA) are covered by the UK's adequacy regulations.

Please contact us if you would like further information about the specific safeguard used when we transfer your personal information outside the UK.

6. Keeping your personal information secure

We have appropriate security measures to prevent your information being accidentally lost, used or accessed in an unauthorised way, altered or disclosed, and limit access to those with a business need-to-know who are subject to a duty of confidentiality. We have procedures to deal with any suspected data security breach and will notify you and any applicable regulator where legally required.

7. How long will you keep my information for?

We keep your information no longer than necessary for the purposes for which it is processed, generally until the production has been produced and for a period afterwards. Some information, such as production footage of your contribution, will be held indefinitely as we have an ongoing legitimate interest in retaining it.

8. Your rights

You have the right to request access to, correction or erasure of your personal information, to object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing, and to request restriction of processing. To exercise these rights please contact the Managing Director in writing at jake@theconnectedset.com.

9. Who to contact with queries or if you have a complaint

Our Managing Director oversees compliance with this notice; contact jake@theconnectedset.com. If a concern cannot be resolved you have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at https://ico.org.uk/concerns/ or 0303 123 1113. Where your request is about your contribution itself, we or third parties may rely on exemptions relating to journalistic freedom and freedom of expression (Article 85 GDPR and national laws including the UK Data Protection Act).

10. Changes to this Privacy Notice

We may amend this privacy notice at any time.
